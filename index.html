/Common questions

Before you
send a request.

Still unsure? Send the part number anyway — a manager will reply.

Contact us
What should I include in my enquiry?

The exact manufacturer part number, quantity and your email. Your name and company are optional.

How do I know the parts are original?

We check factory packaging and MEAN WELL markings against the datasheet and record lot and date codes for every supply. You receive them with the order documents.

Can I buy and pay on this website?

No. Purchases are agreed with a manager. The website collects enquiries; it does not confirm orders or process payment.

How much does delivery cost, and how long does it take?

Pricing and delivery terms are confirmed individually. Until then, lead time is shown as “to be confirmed”.

Are you an authorized MEAN WELL distributor?

No. Partwise sources MEAN WELL parts through suppliers in China and checks every supply for originality before it ships.

Is the price on the card final?

No. It is an indicative price for 1 pc, excluding VAT and delivery. The final price is in your quote.

What is the minimum quantity?

There is no fixed minimum on the site. Factory packaging is a tube of 14 pcs; the minimum for your order is confirmed in the quote.

Do you issue invoices with VAT?

Invoice details and VAT treatment are stated in each quote.

Can I return parts?

Return terms are stated in each quote before you agree to buy.

What if my component is not listed?

You can still enquire using its exact part number. A manager will check whether supply is possible.
